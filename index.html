🌾 Crop Farming

Getting Started

  • Choose crops suited to your climate and soil.
  • Prepare the soil and use healthy seeds.
  • Plan irrigation, weed control, and nutrient management.
  • Monitor pests and diseases regularly.
  • Harvest and store produce correctly.

Start with a crop-specific plan based on your location, season, and budget.

🐄 Livestock Farming

Getting Started

  • Choose a suitable species and production goal.
  • Provide secure, clean, well-ventilated housing.
  • Use balanced feed and clean drinking water.
  • Follow local veterinary vaccination and biosecurity advice.
  • Keep records of costs, growth, health, and sales.

🐟 Fish Farming

Getting Started

  • Select a suitable fish species and production system.
  • Prepare the pond or tank before stocking.
  • Use healthy fingerlings from a reputable hatchery.
  • Monitor water quality and feeding carefully.
  • Track growth, mortality, expenses, and harvest dates.
